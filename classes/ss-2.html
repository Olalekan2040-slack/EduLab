SS 2
Applied Data, Responsible AI and Digital Entrepreneurship

Open to new learners at this class level. Begins with tool orientation; neither SS1 Python nor previous EduLab classes are required.

Age 16–17 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Tools Spreadsheets & AI Literacy Tools
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Learners complete three term projects, store their work in a class portfolio, and explain the safety and practical skills used. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

Term outcome: Clean, summarise and communicate simple data.
1

Data questions

Choose an education or community question.

2

Data tables

Open CSV data in spreadsheet; learn rows, fields and types.

3

Cleaning

Fix missing values, formats and duplicates.

4

Statistics

Calculate sums, averages, percentages.

5

Charts

Choose and produce suitable visuals.

6

Interpretation

Distinguish patterns from causal claims.

7

Storytelling

Build concise report with chart descriptions and source.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 1 Assessed Project

Nigeria Data Story

Data report with dataset source, charts and three findings.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Understand AI capabilities, limits and ethical use through accessible tools.
1

What AI can and cannot do

Discuss pattern recognition and examples in Nigeria.

2

Data and models

Explain training data, examples and bias simply.

3

Prompts and evaluation

Write precise questions; compare outputs.

4

Verification

Check generated facts against trusted sources.

5

Safety and privacy

Avoid personal/sensitive data; discuss plagiarism.

6

AI in education

Explore permitted offline or supervised AI examples.

7

Prepare guided project

Document input, output, checks and human contribution.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 2 Assessed Project

AI-Assisted Study Resource

Fact-checked learning guide with prompts, evidence and AI-use disclosure.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Develop and pitch a small socially useful technology concept.
1

Problem discovery

Interview safely and select local problem.

2

User research

Record three anonymised needs.

3

Solution planning

Define features and project constraints.

4

Prototype

Make a simple site, dashboard or clickable mockup.

5

Cost and pricing

Estimate costs and sources of sustainability.

6

Testing and iteration

Collect feedback; record improvements.

7

Pitch preparation

Prepare demo, impact story and clear next steps.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 3 Assessed Project

Community Tech Solution Pitch

Prototype/mockup, one-page business model, budget and pitch presentation.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Annual portfolio requirement: Three finished projects (editable files where available), three final exports or screenshots, a weekly skills checklist, and a short learner reflection on what they can now do independently.
