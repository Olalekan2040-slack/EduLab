Primary 1–2
Hello, Computer!

Confidence and curiosity — remove fear of the machine.

Age 5–8 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

No theory-only sessions — every week ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

1

Meet the Computer

Learn parts of a computer: monitor, keyboard, mouse, CPU, speaker. Name each part and draw a labelled diagram.

✓ Can name every computer part
2

Switching On & Off

Practice switching on, logging in, shutting down safely. Rules for handling lab equipment.

✓ Operates lab PC independently
3

Mouse Mastery

Mouse control games: single click, double-click, right-click, drag and drop.

✓ Uses mouse confidently
4

The Keyboard

Home row keys, spacebar, backspace, Enter. Type full name for the first time using correct finger placement.

✓ Types own name unaided
5

Keyboard Practice

Type a short sentence about favourite animal. Change font colour/size in Notepad or Word.

✓ Types a full sentence
6

MS Paint — Drawing

Draw family or school using shapes, lines, colours, fill tool.

✓ Creates a digital drawing
7

Saving My Work

Save a file, name it, create a folder, find and reopen saved work.

✓ Saves and reopens own files
8

Term 1 Review

Class quiz on computer parts and keyboard; typing speed test (WPM).

✓ Passes oral and typing quiz
End of Term 1 Project

My Digital Poster

A coloured drawing of themselves with name and school name, created in MS Paint. Printed and taken home.

1

Microsoft Word — Open & Type

Type a short story (3–5 sentences) about favourite day; save with correct file name.

✓ Opens Word and types a story
2

Text Formatting

Bold, italic, underline, font, size, colour; format Week 1 story.

✓ Formats text independently
3

Insert a Picture

Insert clip art or saved image; resize and reposition.

✓ Inserts and positions image
4

Scratch Jr — Characters

Intro to sprites; move a character in 4 directions.

✓ Moves a sprite on screen
5

Scratch Jr — Talking

Speech bubbles; add background scene.

✓ Adds dialogue to animation
6

Scratch Jr — Story

Two characters interact; add sound; simple story arc.

✓ Creates a 3-scene animation
7

Product Design Intro

What is design? Examine everyday objects (chair, pencil case, lunchbox); draw a better version.

✓ Understands design thinking basics
8

Term 2 Project Showcase

Present animated story to classmates; vote for most creative.

✓ Presents work to peers
End of Term 2 Project

My Animated Story

A Scratch Jr. animation with character, dialogue, background, sound. Presented to classmates on the last day.

1

What is the Internet?

Uses of internet; open a browser; what is a URL; typing a web address.

✓ Opens a website independently
2

Searching Safely

Google search for favourite animal; discuss what's safe to click.

✓ Conducts a safe search
3

Online Safety

What is personal information (name, address, phone); role-play scenarios.

✓ Identifies what NOT to share
4

Typing Practice

TypingClub, home row keys; type without looking; timed exercises.

✓ Types using home row keys
5

Typing Speed Challenge

Class typing competition; words-per-minute count.

✓ Improves typing speed
6

Design Your Dream Gadget

Draw and describe an imagined gadget: function, user, appearance, labelled parts.

✓ Applies design thinking to imagination
7

Year Review & Quiz

Fun quiz over all 3 terms: computer parts, mouse, keyboard, internet safety, design.

✓ Demonstrates full year knowledge
8

Graduation Day

Show parents best work of the year (MS Paint drawing, Word story, Dream Gadget design).

✓ Presents digital portfolio to family
End of Term 3 Project

My Internet Report + Dream Gadget

A Word document with a picture and 3 sentences about something found online, plus a hand-drawn labelled dream gadget design.
