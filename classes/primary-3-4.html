Primary 3–4
I Make Things With Tech

Real productivity tools and first creative computing.

Age 8–11 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

No theory-only sessions — every week ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

1

Word Processing Review

Alignment, line spacing, indentation; format a class timetable.

✓ Formats a document independently
2

Tables in Word

Insert a table; weekly timetable with subject, day, time, teacher columns.

✓ Creates and edits tables
3

Headers & Footers

School name in header, page number in footer, page border.

✓ Uses headers, footers, page borders
4

Lists & Text Boxes

Bullets and numbered lists; "Top 10 Things I Love"; text box.

✓ Uses lists and text boxes
5

Shapes & Clipart

Insert shapes, arrows, clipart; design a birthday invitation card.

✓ Designs a card using Word tools
6

Print Preview & Page Setup

Margins, orientation, paper size, print preview.

✓ Prepares document for printing
7

Newspaper Layout

Columns, headline, image, body text — one-page school newspaper front page.

✓ Creates a multi-column layout
8

Term 1 Showcase

Exchange newspapers; peer-review formatting and content.

✓ Gives and receives feedback
End of Term 1 Project

My School Newspaper Front Page

A formatted Word document with headline, two-column article body, inserted image, byline, and page border.

1

Intro to Spreadsheets

Excel or Sheets basics: rows, columns, cells; enter names and scores.

✓ Navigates spreadsheet interface
2

SUM Formula

The SUM function; the = sign; cell references.

✓ Writes and uses SUM correctly
3

AVERAGE, MAX, MIN

Class average, highest, and lowest scores.

✓ Uses 3 functions independently
4

Cell Formatting

Background colour, border, bold headers, centred text.

✓ Formats cells for readability
5

Bar Chart

Bar chart of each student's total score, with title and axis labels.

✓ Creates a labelled bar chart
6

Pie Chart

Class survey (favourite subject); pie chart with percentages.

✓ Creates a pie chart from survey data
7

Product Design — Problem Solving

Redesign a too-heavy school bag; sketch 3 designs, pick best.

✓ Applies iterative design process
8

Data Story Presentation

Present class results chart; explain data in 2 minutes.

✓ Presents data with explanation
End of Term 2 Project

Class Results Analyser

A spreadsheet with 10 student names, scores in 3 subjects, calculated average and grade, and a bar chart.

1

Intro to Canva

Create a free account; explore templates; design a birthday card.

✓ Creates first Canva design
2

Canva — School Flyer

Flyer for a school event; text, image, colours, logo.

✓ Produces a polished flyer
3

Google Slides Intro

Title slide plus 3 content slides on favourite topic.

✓ Builds a basic presentation
4

Transitions & Animations

Slide transitions and text animations; when they help versus distract.

✓ Uses animations purposefully
5

Presenting Skills

Eye contact, clear speech, pointing to slides; 2-minute presentation each.

✓ Delivers a confident 2-minute talk
6

Product Design — Packaging

Design packaging for a product (snack or toy); colour, shape, text, audience.

✓ Designs product packaging with audience in mind
7

Design Review

Share packaging design with a partner; "star" and "wish" feedback.

✓ Gives and receives design feedback
8

End of Year Portfolio

Compile 3 best pieces (newspaper, spreadsheet chart, Canva design) into a Slides portfolio deck.

✓ Curates a digital portfolio
End of Term 3 Project

My Canva Portfolio

3 completed Canva designs (birthday card, event flyer, product packaging), saved and presented.
