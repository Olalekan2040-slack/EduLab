Primary 5
Creative Coding with Scratch

Open to new learners at this class level. A fresh introduction to visual programming; no prior coding needed.

Age 10–11 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Tools Scratch (Offline)
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Learners complete three term projects, store their work in a class portfolio, and explain the safety and practical skills used. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

Term outcome: Understand sequences, sprites, events and movement.
1

Digital safety and Scratch

Open Scratch offline/editor and save a starter project.

2

Algorithm and sequence

Write step-by-step instructions for a daily activity.

3

Stage and sprites

Choose, draw and move characters.

4

Events and motion

Use when-green-flag and motion blocks.

5

Looks and speech

Create dialogue, costumes and backdrops.

6

Timing and sound

Use waits, sequences and permitted sound.

7

Build and debug

Combine scenes and test story flow.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 1 Assessed Project

Interactive Animated Story

Scratch animation with 2 scenes, 2 characters and narration/text.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Use conditions, sensing and repetition to build interactive behaviour.
1

Review in this term

Create a small Scratch script from scratch.

2

Loops

Repeat actions with repeat and forever blocks.

3

Conditions

Make if/else decisions using simple examples.

4

Sensing and input

Ask questions and receive typed answers.

5

Variables

Create a score and reset it.

6

Feedback

Display correct/incorrect responses.

7

Test with classmates

Fix scoring, question flow and restart logic.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 2 Assessed Project

Interactive Quiz

Scratch quiz with at least 5 questions, feedback and restart.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Combine events, logic and variables in a playable game.
1

Mini-game introduction

Build a simple moving sprite and save.

2

Player controls

Use arrow keys or mouse to move a character.

3

Objects and randomness

Position objects using random coordinates.

4

Collision detection

Detect touching and respond.

5

Score and time

Use variables for score or countdown.

6

Rules and levels

Create goal, instructions and end state.

7

Debug and polish

Peer-test, improve difficulty and document controls.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 3 Assessed Project

Catch-the-Star Game

Playable game with scoring, instructions and win/lose condition.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Annual portfolio requirement: Three finished projects (editable files where available), three final exports or screenshots, a weekly skills checklist, and a short learner reflection on what they can now do independently.
