JSS 3
Data Literacy, Spreadsheets and Databases

Open to new learners at this class level. Self-contained data course; no prior Python or spreadsheets expected.

Age 14–15 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Tools LibreOffice Calc & Base
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Learners complete three term projects, store their work in a class portfolio, and explain the safety and practical skills used. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

Term outcome: Enter, clean and calculate classroom data.
1

Data and spreadsheet basics

Open spreadsheet, cells, rows, columns, save.

2

Data entry

Create a small fictional result sheet.

3

Formulas

Use +, -, SUM and AVERAGE.

4

References

Apply formulas using relative references.

5

Sorting and filtering

Locate highest and lowest results.

6

Charts

Create a bar chart from aggregated totals.

7

Check errors

Verify formulas and protect private student data.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 1 Assessed Project

Class Results Workbook

Workbook with scores, totals, averages and grade summary.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Turn a small dataset into useful, honest charts.
1

Refresh spreadsheet basics

Use a starter dataset and cell formulas.

2

Collect and define data

Create safe multiple-choice survey questions.

3

Data quality

Find blanks, duplicate entries and inconsistencies.

4

Counts and categories

Use COUNTIF and simple pivot-style summaries.

5

Charts for purpose

Select column/bar or pie chart appropriately.

6

Interpret results

Write three cautious insights.

7

Create dashboard

Organise charts, sources and explanatory captions.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 2 Assessed Project

School Survey Dashboard

Survey spreadsheet with 20+ fictional responses, two charts and findings.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Use tables, fields and forms to organise information.
1

Database concepts

Understand records, fields, tables and keys.

2

Plan safe dataset

Use invented student names and IDs.

3

Create tables

Define appropriate field types.

4

Data entry forms

Add records using a simple form.

5

Queries and filters

Find members by club or level.

6

Reports

Create a simple membership report.

7

Data protection and testing

Check duplicates, access and backup.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 3 Assessed Project

School Club Member Register

LibreOffice Base/Access or spreadsheet-backed database with queries.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Annual portfolio requirement: Three finished projects (editable files where available), three final exports or screenshots, a weekly skills checklist, and a short learner reflection on what they can now do independently.
