Primary 4
Digital Art, Design & Document Production

Digital art, document design, and offline research skills.

Age 9–10 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Software Tux Paint & LibreOffice Writer
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Designed for offline delivery on shared lab computers — no theory-only sessions, every week ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

1

Tux Paint Interface

Canvas layout, tool selectors, color palette, undo.

2

Lines, Brushes & Shapes

Building architectural shapes like houses and trees.

3

Color Theory & Fill Limits

Border enclosures and Fill Bucket logic.

4

Visual Stamps & Alignment

Scaling, flipping, and placing stamp objects.

5

Magic Tools & Filters

Applying Grass, Brick, Rainbow, and blur effects.

6

Typography in Art

Selecting fonts, sizing titles, placing labels over art.

7

Poster Layout Principles

Margins, balance, white space, visual contrast.
