Primary 4
Digital Research and Publishing

Open to new learners at this class level. Begins with essential computer and typing practice; no Primary 3 completion required.

Age 9–10 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Tools LibreOffice Writer
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Learners complete three term projects, store their work in a class portfolio, and explain the safety and practical skills used. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

Term outcome: Use drawing and page-layout tools to communicate a clear message.
1

Starter computer skills

Practise mouse, keyboard, open/save and lab rules.

2

Purpose and audience

Identify what makes a poster clear and useful.

3

Create headings

Type a short title and subheadings in Writer.

4

Page layout

Use margins, spacing, alignment and readable fonts.

5

Pictures and shapes

Insert or draw original icons and pictures.

6

Colour and hierarchy

Use contrast and visual order responsibly.

7

Revise and export

Proofread, peer-review, save PDF.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 1 Assessed Project

Classroom Rules Poster

A4 poster, planning sketch and final PDF.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Research safely using curated offline/online sources and write factual text.
1

Computer review and safety

Open files, rename and practise safe browsing.

2

Research questions

Choose one animal and draft four questions.

3

Finding reliable facts

Use teacher-curated Kiwix/offline encyclopaedia or safe sites.

4

Notes in own words

Record short facts without copying long paragraphs.

5

Tables and labels

Create a habitat/food/lifespan table.

6

Images and credits

Insert permitted image and name its source.

7

Draft and review

Build and edit a two-page fact sheet.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 2 Assessed Project

Animal Fact Sheet

Two-page fact sheet with image, table, sources and PDF.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Plan and publish a simple evidence-based newsletter.
1

What is a newsletter?

Examine headings, articles, captions and sections.

2

Interview safely

Prepare questions and interview a classmate or teacher.

3

Report local information

Write a short school/community story.

4

Photo and caption

Use permitted photographs or drawings with captions.

5

Create page layout

Place titles, text blocks and pictures.

6

Check facts and consent

Verify names and do not publish private information.

7

Finish publication

Edit, combine stories and export final PDF.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 3 Assessed Project

My Community Newsletter

Two-page illustrated school/community newsletter and PDF.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Annual portfolio requirement: Three finished projects (editable files where available), three final exports or screenshots, a weekly skills checklist, and a short learner reflection on what they can now do independently.
