Primary 3
Computer Confidence, Typing and Digital Storytelling

Open to new learners at this class level. No prior computer experience required. Learners gain confidence using a PC, produce a document, and tell a story digitally.

Age 8–9 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term Tools LibreOffice Writer, Digital Painting App
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

Learners complete three term projects, store their work in a class portfolio, and explain the safety and practical skills used. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

Term outcome: Identify and operate the main parts of a computer safely.
1

Meet the computer

Identify monitor, system unit, keyboard, mouse and headphones.

2

Lab rules and care

Practise safe handling, posture, sharing and basic safety.

3

Switch on and shut down

Follow the correct startup, login and shutdown sequence.

4

Mouse control

Point, click, double-click, drag and drop in a practice activity.

5

Keyboard basics

Type letters, numbers, space, enter and backspace.

6

Files and folders

Create a named folder and save a teacher-provided file.

7

Prepare portfolio

Combine an illustrated diagram and a skills checklist.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 1 Assessed Project

My Computer Skills Portfolio

Computer-parts diagram, safe-start checklist, saved practice file.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Type, format and save a simple school document.
1

Open a word processor

Start LibreOffice Writer or equivalent and type a name.

2

Typing sentences

Enter short sentences with capitals and punctuation.

3

Edit text

Select, delete, correct and move text.

4

Format text

Change title size, bold, colour and alignment.

5

Lists and spacing

Create a clear list of school activities.

6

Insert simple table

Build a two-column daily timetable.

7

Save and export

Save an editable copy and create a PDF.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 2 Assessed Project

My School Routine

One-page document with title, list, timetable and PDF.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Term outcome: Make original digital illustrations and a short story.
1

Open painting software

Find brushes, lines, colours and erase tools.

2

Draw shapes

Draw a school, tree or home using basic shapes.

3

Plan a story

Identify character, setting and beginning/middle/end.

4

Make character images

Draw and save two original character pictures.

5

Combine pictures and text

Put one image and short text on each page.

6

Improve readability

Check spelling, alignment, font size and contrast.

7

Export storybook

Save source files and export the booklet to PDF.

8

Term project and showcase

Complete, test, present and submit the term project.

End of Term 3 Assessed Project

My Digital Storybook

Three-page illustrated story with opening, middle and ending.

Assessment checks: Functional or complete output; accurate key skills; file organisation; explanation; safety and originality.

Annual portfolio requirement: Three finished projects (editable files where available), three final exports or screenshots, a weekly skills checklist, and a short learner reflection on what they can now do independently.
