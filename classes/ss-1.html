I Build Real Products

Full-stack foundations and WAEC/JAMB readiness.

Age 15–16 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

No theory-only sessions — every week ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

APIs

Web Scraping

Automation

Password Generator

Personal Automation Tool

Flask Introduction

Flask Forms

Notice Board Web App
