Primary 5–6
I Communicate and Create Online

Digital citizenship, professional tools, and first programming.

Age 11–13 years 3 Terms 8 Weeks per Term
Term-by-Term Curriculum

Every Week,
Something Built.

No theory-only sessions — every week ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Select a term to see its full breakdown.

1

How the Internet Works

Servers, websites, URLs explained via diagram; label how a webpage loads.

✓ Explains how the internet works
2

Gmail — Create & Compose

Create an account; compose an intro email with subject, greeting, body, sign-off.

✓ Sends a properly formatted email
3

Email Etiquette

Professional tone, CC/BCC, reply vs reply-all; role-play emailing a teacher.

✓ Applies professional email tone
4

Attachments & Labels

Attach a Word document; organise inbox with labels and stars; delete and archive.

✓ Manages inbox professionally
5

Online Research Skills

Evaluate 3 websites on the same topic for trustworthiness (author, date, source).

✓ Evaluates online sources critically
6

Spotting Fake News

Identify fake headlines; red flags such as no author, exaggerated language, poor spelling.

✓ Identifies misinformation online
7

Digital Citizenship

What you post stays forever; what's okay to share; potential harms.

✓ Makes responsible digital choices
8

Email Portfolio

Write and send 5 emails: introduction, question, thank-you, apology, follow-up.

✓ Produces 5-email professional portfolio
End of Term 1 Project

Professional Email Portfolio

Five emails: introduce yourself, ask a question, follow up, thank someone, report a problem.

1

What is Programming?

Algorithms in real life (recipe, morning routine); write step-by-step tea instructions.

✓ Understands what an algorithm is
2

Scratch — Sprites & Events

Sprites, stage, scripts, events; move a character on keypress.

✓ Creates first Scratch script
3

Scratch — Score Variable

Score variable; increases when touching a star; display on screen.

✓ Uses variables in Scratch
4

Scratch — Conditions

"You Win!" at score 10; "Game Over" on falling; add lives.

✓ Uses if/else conditions
5

Scratch — Sound & Music

Sound effects, background music, custom recorded sound.

✓ Adds audio to a project
6

Scratch — Test & Debug

Swap games with a partner; find and fix bugs; discuss importance of testing.

✓ Debugs another student's code
7

Product Design — User Research

Ask 5 classmates their biggest school problem; identify most common.

✓ Conducts simple user research
8

Games Festival

Present Scratch game; classmates vote most fun, most creative, best design.

✓ Presents completed interactive game
End of Term 2 Project

My Scratch Game

A playable game with character, score variable, win/lose condition, sound, and 3+ levels or obstacles.

1

Google Drive

Upload, organise, share files; shared folder; view, comment, and edit access differences.

✓ Manages and shares files in Drive
2

Google Docs — Collaboration

Two students co-write a story live; comments and suggestions.

✓ Collaborates on a live document
3

Google Sheets — Shared Budget

Class shared party budget; items with cost; auto total.

✓ Contributes to a shared spreadsheet
4

Google Forms — Create a Survey

5-question survey; send to 10+ classmates, collect responses.

✓ Builds and distributes a survey
5

Analyse Survey Results

View responses in Sheets; most popular option; chart from data.

✓ Analyses survey data
6

Product Design — Prototype

Based on Term 2 research, sketch a solution; paper or Canva prototype.

✓ Creates a prototype from research
7

Design Pitch

90-second pitch: problem, solution, audience.

✓ Pitches a product idea
8

Year-End Showcase

Present survey report (Slides), Scratch game, and product prototype to parents.

✓ Presents full year work to an audience
End of Term 3 Project

Class Survey Report

A Google Form survey (10+ responses), Sheets analysis, a Slides findings deck, plus a paper or Canva prototype.
