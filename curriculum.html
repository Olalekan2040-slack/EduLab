Federal Curriculum Covered.
Class by Class.

Every topic the NERDC mandates from Primary 1 through SS3, structured across three terms per class, eight teaching weeks per term, and built to run entirely offline on a shared lab of computers. Every session ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Choose a class below to see its full term-by-term breakdown.

Every class runs 3 terms of 8 teaching weeks each, on free and open-source software chosen to run offline across a shared lab of 20 computers. Every student receives a course manual, an N-TECH/EduLab certificate, and entry into the Annual EduLab Hackathon.
