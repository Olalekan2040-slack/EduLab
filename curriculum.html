Class by Class.

Every topic the NERDC mandates from Primary 1 through SS2, structured across three terms per class, eight teaching weeks per term. Every session ends with something the student made, built, typed, or designed. Choose a class below to see its full term-by-term breakdown.

Every class runs 3 terms of 8 teaching weeks each. Product Design thinking — user research, sketching, prototyping, testing, and pitching — runs as a thread through every level. Every student receives a course manual, an N-TECH/EduLab certificate, and entry into the Annual EduLab Hackathon.
