Digital Skills Curriculum.
Class by Class.

Nine self-contained annual courses from Primary 3 through SS2, each an independent 24-week programme — three terms of eight teaching weeks, one assessed practical project per term, built to run entirely offline on a shared school computer lab. Choose a class below to see its full term-by-term breakdown.
