The Model

Simple. Sustainable.
Built to Change Lives.

No capital outlay. No hidden costs. A genuine partnership where schools win, students gain world-class tech skills, and EduLab earns its keep.

1
Selection

We Find Your School

We identify private schools in Ondo and Osun States without a functional ICT lab. You apply — we visit, assess your space, and confirm the partnership within days.

2
Agreement

We Sign a 5-Year BOT Deal

You give us one room and mains power. We install 20 to 50 computers (scaled to your student population), UPS backup, networking, projector, and all software — within 30 days, at no cost to you.

3
Free Use

Your School Uses the Lab Freely

The equipment belongs to EduLab — but your school uses the computers freely during non-training hours for lessons, school exams, admin, or any purpose. It becomes your school's ICT facility in every practical sense.

4
Training

We Train Every Student

Certified EduLab instructors deliver structured tech training per class per week — from Scratch and Python to full-stack web development — building real, employable skills alongside intensive CBT practice for JAMB, WAEC, and NECO.

5
Revenue

Students Pay a Small Term Contribution

Students contribute a modest term fee, far less than a textbook bundle — that contribution is EduLab's revenue. The school gains a world-class lab and trained students at zero cost, always.

6
Outcome

Students Gain a Real, Lasting Edge

By SS3, EduLab graduates have built real full-stack web applications and backend systems, sat hundreds of CBT mock exams, and hold a verified digital skills certificate — positioning them for tech jobs, freelance income, and global remote opportunities that no other school in their city offers.

Curriculum

Federal Curriculum Covered.
Real Skills Built.

Every topic the NERDC mandates from Primary 1 through SS3 — delivered offline on free, open-source software with CBT exam preparation built into every level. Each class has its own dedicated, term-by-term curriculum.

Primary 1–2

Hello, Computer!

Confidence and curiosity — mouse, keyboard, MS Paint, first animations.

Primary 3

Computer Fundamentals & Digital Literacy

Hardware, touch typing, and first word-processed documents.

Primary 4

Digital Art, Design & Document Production

Tux Paint design, intermediate documents, and offline research.

Primary 5

Visual Programming & Block Logic I

Scratch animation, sensing logic, and a first platformer game.

Primary 6

Advanced Game Architecture & Systems

Variables, cloning, and lists, building to a capstone Scratch game.

JSS 1

Fundamentals of IT & Office Applications

Word processing, spreadsheets, and presentations in LibreOffice.

JSS 2

Advanced Spreadsheets, Databases & Algorithms

Logical formulas, relational databases, and flowcharting into code.

JSS 3

Web Foundations & Computer Networks

HTML5, CSS3 and Flexbox, plus networking and cybersecurity basics.

SS 1

Python Programming & Data Analytics

Core Python, data structures, and a first analytics project.

SS 2

Web Engineering, Databases & ORM

JavaScript, relational SQL databases, and Python ORM integration.

SS 3

Backend Systems, Security & Capstone

Flask/Django, Git, software security, and a capstone school system.

Who Qualifies

Is Your School
a Good Fit?

Accepting applications from schools in Ondo and Osun States. Here is what we look for.

1

Private School

Any registered private primary, secondary, or combined school is eligible — regardless of size.

2

100+ Students

At least 100 enrolled students ensures the programme works financially for both parties.

3

No Functional ICT Lab

Schools without a working lab are our first priority. Outdated labs may also qualify.

4

One Room + Power

A dedicated room (at least 40sqm) and mains power. We bring UPS backup — NEPA is not an excuse.

5

Ondo or Osun State

Deploying now in both states. Other Southwest schools can join the expansion waitlist.

6

Committed Proprietor

A 5-year BOT partnership requires genuine leadership buy-in. Schools that are serious rise to the top of our list.
